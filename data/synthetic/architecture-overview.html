Payments platform — architecture overview

The payments platform has four core services. api-gateway authenticates merchants and applies rate limits. payment-api accepts card payments and asks ledger-service to record each transaction.

ledger-service is the system of record. It writes to the primary Postgres cluster through PgBouncer and, after each successful write, publishes an event to the Kafka topic payments.settled.

notification-service consumes payments.settled and sends receipt e-mails and merchant webhooks. If ledger-service is slow, payment confirmations and receipts are delayed.
