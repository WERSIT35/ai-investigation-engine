Incident report INC-1057 — Delayed merchant webhooks

On 2026-03-18 merchants reported that webhooks for settled payments arrived up to 3 hours late. Payment processing itself was not affected.

Root cause: on March 10 the payments.settled topic was expanded from 12 to 24 partitions (CHG-3310) but notification-service was not redeployed, so its consumers only read the original 12 partitions. Events on the new partitions accumulated as consumer lag.

Fix: notification-service was redeployed with rebalancing enabled on 2026-03-20 (CHG-3325). Lag cleared within one hour.

Affected service: notification-service.
